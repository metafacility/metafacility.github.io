Rigaku Ultima IV

A high-performance, multipurpose powder X-ray diffractometer for phase identification, crystallinity, lattice parameter and crystallite size analysis of powders, bulk solids and thin films.

Make / ModelRigaku Ultima IV
X-ray SourceCu K-alpha, λ = 1.5406 Å (sealed tube)
Generator RatingUp to 40 kV / 30 mA (1.2 kW)
GeometryBragg-Brentano (θ/2θ), parallel beam optics for thin films
GoniometerTheta–theta, vertical, wide-angle
Standard 2θ Range5° – 90°
DetectorScintillation counter / high-speed detector
Typical Scan ParametersStep 0.02°, scan speed 10°/min

Powder Samples

Particle SizeFinely ground, ≤ 50 µm
QuantityMinimum powder volume ~1.5 ml

Solid / Film Samples

DimensionsUp to 20 mm × 20 mm, thickness ≤ 5 mm
SurfaceFlat and well polished
