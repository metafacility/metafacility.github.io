Results & Receipts

Follow the procedure below to receive your results.

  1. Pay the XRD charges

    Pay the usage charges to the institute account.

    View institute account details →
  2. Upload the payment screenshot

    Submit a screenshot showing the transaction reference number using the upload form on the next page.

  3. Fill in the transaction details

    Submit the Microsoft Form with your transaction details after uploading your screenshot.

  4. Receive your results by e-mail

    After the operator verifies your payment, the results will be e-mailed to the webmail ID used to book the XRD slot within one working day.

⏳ Didn't receive your results within one working day? Contact the instrument operator at azhagappan@nitt.edu.
