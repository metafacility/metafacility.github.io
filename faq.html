Frequently Asked Questions

Sample preparation, submission and data delivery

Sample Preparation Guidelines

How should powder samples be prepared?

Grind the sample to a fine powder (≤ 50 µm) and submit a minimum volume of about 1.5 ml. Coarse or uneven particles cause preferred orientation and poor peak intensities.

What are the requirements for solid samples and films?

The surface must be flat and well polished, up to 20 mm × 20 mm with thickness ≤ 5 mm.

How should I label and pack my samples?

Pack each sample in a ziplock pouch labelled with the Applicant Name exactly as entered in the booking form.

Which samples are not accepted?

Corrosive, toxic, radioactive, volatile or hygroscopic-unsealed materials are not accepted.

When and where do I drop off samples?

Submit samples physically at the facility before the deadline mentioned in the confirmation e-mail. Late samples roll over to the next session.

How many samples can I book?

Each session is capped at 24 samples, so a per-user limit applies to keep slots fair for everyone.

Data Delivery

In what format will I receive my data?

Raw data is supplied as .raw and .txt (2θ vs. intensity) files.

How long does data delivery take?

Data is e-mailed to the address given in the booking form, normally within 1–2 working days after the session.

Software Resources

Which software can I use to analyse my data?
  • Origin / Excel — plotting the .txt data.
  • VESTA — visualising crystal structures.
  • Match! / HighScore / Profex — phase identification.
  • FullProf / GSAS-II — Rietveld refinement.
