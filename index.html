Submission Guidelines & FAQ

Sample Labeling Format

Label each sample and submit in a ziplock pouch with Applicant Name, the label must match the name entered in the booking form exactly.

Physical Drop-off Deadlines

Samples must be physically submitted at the facility before the deadline mentioned in the e-mail. Late samples roll over to the next available session.

Maximum Samples per User

Each user may book a limited number of samples per session so that all 24 slots are shared fairly.

Raw Data Delivery

Raw data files (.raw / .txt) are emailed to the address provided in the booking form, normally within 1–2 working days after the session. Please keep a copy of your booking confirmation.
