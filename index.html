Submission Guidelines & FAQ

Sample Labeling Format

Label each sample holder/vial as SupervisorInitials_UserName_SampleNo (e.g. AK_Ravi_03). The label must match the name entered in the booking form exactly.

Physical Drop-off Deadlines

Samples must be physically submitted at the facility before the deadline announced in the ticker (typically Tuesday 4:00 PM before the session). Late samples roll over to the next available session.

Maximum Samples per User

Each user may book a limited number of samples per session so that all 24 slots are shared fairly. Check the booking form for the current per-user limit.

Raw Data Delivery

Raw data files (.raw / .xy / .csv) are emailed to the address provided in the booking form, normally within 2–3 working days after the session. Please keep a copy of your booking confirmation.
